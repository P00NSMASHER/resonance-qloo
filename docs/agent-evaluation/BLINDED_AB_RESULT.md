# Blinded anchor-only versus Qloo-expanded proxy evaluation

Classification: synthetic agent evaluation—not real-user evidence.

Five isolated evaluators received the two complete plans as Plan A and Plan B, with presentation order alternated. Labels did not reveal the generation method. Every eligible result was retained.

| Evaluator | Anchor-only mean | Qloo-expanded mean | Preference |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| AB-01 | 4.4/5 | 3.2/5 | Anchor-only |
| AB-02 | 4.4/5 | 3.4/5 | Anchor-only |
| AB-03 | 4.4/5 | 3.6/5 | Anchor-only |
| AB-04 | 4.4/5 | 4.2/5 | Anchor-only |
| AB-05 | 4.4/5 | 4.4/5 | Tie |

Exact preference result: Qloo-expanded 0/5 (0%); anchor-only 4/5 (80%); tie 1/5 (20%). The result is unfavorable and is intentionally retained. Reviewers consistently found the Qloo plan more novel, but found `Reporter` and `Inventive` strained or insufficiently grounded and preferred the anchor-only plan's coherence and immediate usability.

This does not establish user impact. It identifies a product limitation: raw adjacent signals can add novelty without improving the whole plan. Repairs therefore focus on concrete mechanics, transparent provenance, and facilitator control; they do not relabel the result or selectively remove returned signals. The canonical replacement gate's required 70% blinded win was not met.
