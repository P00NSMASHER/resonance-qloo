## Whole-output rubric

Three isolated reviewers score the complete returned evidence and resulting plan:

- exact resolution: 0–5;
- intuitive cross-category relationship: 0–5;
- visual clarity: 0–5;
- activity usefulness: 0–5;
- low semantic weirdness: 0–5;
- meaningful surprise: 0–5.

Overall score is the unweighted mean multiplied by 20.

## Replacement gate

Ella Fitzgerald + Roman Holiday remains canonical unless one fixed candidate:

1. completes with exact resolution;
2. scores at least 98/100;
3. has no selected signal that any reviewer says needs a strained explanation;
4. keeps Qloo visibly indispensable;
5. receives unanimous reviewer approval;
6. wins at least 70% of a separate blinded proxy comparison;
7. is materially stronger as a complete output, with no hidden or removed signal.

Failure to clear any gate means no replacement. All complete outputs and review records remain public.
